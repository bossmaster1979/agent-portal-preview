How the portal works

  • A buyer answers six readiness questions online and says yes to a call.
  • Only buyers who passed the checks reach the board: do-not-call list checked, call + text + email consent recorded, not opted out.
  • An agent sees the area, budget range, timeline, and pre-approval — never the name or phone number — until they claim that one buyer.
  • One buyer goes to one agent only. A claimed buyer leaves the board at once and cannot be claimed twice.
  • After a claim, the agent gets the handover sheet and a booked call time.
